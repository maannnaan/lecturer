Lecturer.bd — three design directions

The same three screens in three looks: a teacher's public website (lecturer.bd/tanvir), a student's chapter page with MCQ and CQ math, and the teacher dashboard. The teacher, students and numbers are made up. Pick one direction, or mix (e.g. "A's colours with B's dashboard menu"). Everything after this is built on the pick.

  • Live pages: click a thumbnail's "open" link; the dark switcher at the bottom-left flips A / B / C and pages.
  • Phone view: each "phone" image shows the whole 360px-wide page in columns.
